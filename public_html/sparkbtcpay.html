About the Project

Spark BTC Pay is an open-source education initiative that teaches practical Lightning Network usage for recurring payments and memberships in Argentina — a country leading LATAM in Bitcoin adoption due to persistent inflation.

We combine:

  • Real-world Lightning payments (any wallet → Wallet of Satoshi)
  • AI tools to transform business models and client services
  • Community-driven onboarding through consultants

All educational materials, guides, templates and integration code will be released under open-source licenses (MIT / CC BY) on GitHub — contributing directly to Bitcoin and Lightning ecosystem growth.

The Cascading Effect

Our community members are consultants who already serve other businesses.

By mastering Lightning payments and AI transformation through Spark BTC Pay, they:

  1. Start receiving and paying in satoshis themselves
  2. Teach their clients how to do the same
  3. Onboard those clients into the Bitcoin/Lightning economy

This creates a powerful network effect: one consultant can influence dozens or hundreds of businesses — accelerating real, sustainable adoption in LATAM and beyond.

Key Features & Deliverables

Lightning Subscriptions

Recurring satoshi payments via LNURL / BTCPay Server — from any Lightning wallet to Wallet of Satoshi. Low fees, instant, censorship-resistant.

AI + Business Transformation

Educational sessions showing how AI tools optimize workflows, automate invoicing, predict fees and create new revenue models — all tied to Bitcoin payments.

Open-Source Education

Guides, tutorials, code templates and case studies — all public, free and reusable for the global Bitcoin community.
